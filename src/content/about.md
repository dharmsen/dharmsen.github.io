# About me

I'm a PhD candidate at Eindhoven University of Technology in the
[AMOR/e lab](https://amore-labs.github.io/website), supervised by
[Joaquin Vanschoren](https://joaquinvanschoren.github.io/home/). I am part of
the [OpenEuroLLM](https://openeurollm.eu/) project, which is a European
collaboration between different research organizations to create a family of
sovereign European large language models. 

My research is in multilinguality of large language models.

In my free time, I enjoy [reading](https://www.goodreads.com/user/show/95482565-dalton), [running](https://www.strava.com/athletes/185793448), playing guitar, playing [video](https://steamcommunity.com/id/Raphenwastaken/)/card/board
games.
